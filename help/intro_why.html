Cutting parts

 

I struggle with cutting shapes, even straight lines, with my plasma cutter. Recently, as a fun project, I attempted to make a sleeve hitch plate. The plans called for a 2" diameter washer with a 7/8" center hole cut out of 3/16" thick steel. When I tried to free hand this washer, it wasn't round and it wasn't 2" in diameter. I then tried to use a hole saw. This worked, but it took forever to cut the washer and the size was 2-1/8" because I didn't have a 2" hole saw. This is when I decided I needed a CNC plasma table.

CNC Plasma Tables

 

I looked at several available tables. The price tags for them was well outside my willingness for something I consider a hobby. Even JD's Garage's offering requires significant work in cutting or printing the parts and assembling everything, including the microcontroller.

Laser Engraver Conversion

 

I've watched several YouTube videos of people converting Laser Engravers into CNC plasma tables. However, I have yet to see a video that describes the whole process from beginning to end.

CAD Software

 

The other issue is the CAD software. I didn't want to pay licenses for software that I'm going to use in a hobby. This was the next issue I looked into. The obvious choice is FreeCAD. When I looked into FreeCAD, it was disappointing to find out that the G-Code it (with the help of linuxcnc) puts out does not have the commands in it to turn the torch on and off. Other G-Code commands necessary to run the table are also missing. I decided this is where I needed to start. If I could learn FreeCAD, understand what the changes to the G-Code were necessary for CNC plasma to work, and automate it, then it would be worth while to buy a laser engraver and set it up to work with my plasma cutter.

plasma_post

 

The shortcomings of FreeCAD are actually a blessing. I don't think I'd want FreeCAD producing CNC plasma G-Code. The reason is that you'd constantly be modifying your model, adjusting the thickness (Z-Axis) of your model or part. I wrote "plasma_post" to edit the G-Code that linuxcnc produces. This software will modify the G-Code adding the necessary codes needed to cut the part. Once I could see that this was going to work, I began to spend the money to acquire the laser engraver and getting it hooked up to my plasma cutter.
